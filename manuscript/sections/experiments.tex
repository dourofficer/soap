\paragraph{Datasets.} We evaluate on four failure-attribution benchmarks, each providing per-trajectory annotations of the decisive-error step and the responsible agent: \textbf{Who\&When}~\citep{whoandwhen}, \textbf{CORRECT-Error}~\citep{correct}, and \textbf{ElephantTrace}~\citep{elephant} (denoted \emph{Elephant}); see Appendix~\ref{app:datasets} for details. The four benchmarks span short and long trajectories and QA- and code-centric domains, letting us test whether the contamination correction holds beyond a single length or task regime.
% TODO: confirm exact task names/spellings for CORRECT-Error; confirm citation keys \citep{correct}, \citep{elephant} exist in the .bib.

\paragraph{Metrics.} Following \citet{whoandwhen}, \textbf{Step-Level Accuracy} and \textbf{Agent-Level Accuracy}. We report Step-Level first throughout, meanwhile tolerance ($\pm1,\pm2$) and ranked-set metrics (step@$k$, MRR) are deferred to the appendix.

\paragraph{Baselines.} We compare against, in three families:
(i)~\emph{prompt-based} methods --- \textbf{All-at-Once}, \textbf{Step-by-Step}, \textbf{Binary Search}~\citep{whoandwhen}, the voting method \textbf{ECHO}~\citep{echo}, the schema-retrieval method \textbf{CORRECT}~\citep{correct}, and the dependency-search methods \textbf{FALAT}~\citep{falat} and \textbf{CHIEF}~\citep{chief};
(ii)~a \emph{trained attributor}, \textbf{AgenTracer}~\citep{agentracer}, fine-tuned on annotated failure trajectories;
(iii)~an \emph{adapted step-level verifier}, \textbf{Qwen-PRM}, an off-the-shelf process reward model applied zero-shot as a per-step scorer.
A \textbf{Random} predictor is included as a floor. To ensure a fair comparison, all prompt-based and adapted baselines are reproduced under the same two backbone models used by \crr{} (below), rather than transcribed from their original papers.
% TODO: confirm \citep{falat},\citep{chief} keys; FALAT and CHIEF are concurrent work — decide whether to also flag them as such in Related Work.

\paragraph{Implementation details.} \crr{} reads a proxy language model $\proxy$ that did not generate the trajectories, exposing hidden states and post-softmax attention. We use two backbones throughout, \textbf{Qwen3.5-8B}~\citep{qwen3} and \textbf{DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B}~\citep{deepseekr1}; the same two models serve as the judge/proxy for the reproduced baselines. The SVD reference $R$ is fit on a train split; the discount $\gamma$, attention layer band $L^\star$, and SVD component band $\mathcal{C}$ are selected on a held-out validation split and fixed for test. Scores are ranked within each trajectory (\eqref{eq:prediction}); unless noted we report the single validation-selected configuration, mean over runs.
