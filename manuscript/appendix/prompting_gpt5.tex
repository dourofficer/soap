\section{Prompting Baselines: Full Grid}
\label{app:prompting-gpt5}

Tables~\ref{tab:prompting-gpt4o} and~\ref{tab:prompting-gpt5} report all three
prompting strategies under both judges and both ground-truth settings; accuracy is
computed over the full dataset, as in the main tables. Table~\ref{tab:prompting-gpt4o}
completes the GPT-4o judge used in Tables~\ref{tab:main} and~\ref{tab:main-gt}:
Step-by-Step is the strongest of the three overall in both settings, which is why it
is the strategy carried into Table~\ref{tab:main-gt}. Under the stronger GPT-5 judge
(Table~\ref{tab:prompting-gpt5}) the ranking changes --- Binary Search dominates
every column in both settings, and on CE it exceeds all other methods in the main
tables.
